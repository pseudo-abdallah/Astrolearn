EXPLORE YOUR SKY / OFFLINE
A sky map for your location
Uses the observer location and time from Observe. The outer circle is the horizon; the center is overhead. East is on the left, as when looking up.
Draw the map, then select a star to inspect its predicted position.


BRIGHTNESS OVER TIME / OFFLINE
Explore a light curve
Import a CSV with time,flux columns from your measurements or an exported archive dataset. This viewer normalizes flux by its median; it does not search for planets.
The lesson uses simulated measurements, not a discovered planet. Imported data is saved locally.


OPTIONAL ONLINE LEARNING

These external resources need internet. They do not receive your uploaded image from AstroLearn.
  • Siril — image calibration, stacking and processing.
  • GraXpert — optional background-gradient processing. Keep the original image when importing an edited copy.
  • Lightkurve tutorials — photometry and transit lessons.
  • MAST / TESS data and tutorials · exo.MAST — archived observations and known exoplanets.
  • Cosmoscope · AstroViewer — additional sky exploration.
  • Spartificial notebooks · Telescope comparison article — learning references.
